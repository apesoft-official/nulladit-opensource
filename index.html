Privacy and terms

Privacy notice

Current status: NULLADIT is an experimental preview. The public documentation says blockchain account registration and validator rewards are not operational. This site does not ask for identity documents, and the preview does not promise that every network or hosting operator can avoid processing connection metadata such as an IP address.

Wallet sessions: A wallet signature can show control of a wallet address; it does not establish a person's real-world identity or make a session unrestricted. The design goal is to avoid collecting unnecessary identifiers during authenticated sessions. Network, hosting, and infrastructure providers may still process technical connection data needed to deliver and secure their services, under their own policies.

Vestigium: Vestigium is described in the public repository as research/proposed work, not a deployed production service. Its documented contract concept anchors opaque evidence commitments; it does not put message contents, identity details, IP addresses, or device identifiers on-chain, and it does not itself identify or punish anyone. NULLADIT's constitutional rules call for minimizing data and limiting pool queries; unauthenticated or excessive requests may be challenged, rate-limited, or denied. They are not required to disclose personal identity as a condition of making a public query.

Where account registration is enabled in a future release, any stated $10-equivalent account fee would not by itself prove a person's identity or grant unrestricted access to the Pelavium pool. Account registration is not operational in this public preview.

Read the NULLADIT Constitution, architecture and preview status, Vestigium technical note, Pelavium research repository, and public encryption-logic repository.
